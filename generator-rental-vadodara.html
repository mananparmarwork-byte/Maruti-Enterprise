Generator rental for business, construction and events

Temporary power requirements vary by site. Factories, hospitals, warehouses, construction projects, commercial buildings and events can have different loads, access conditions and operating schedules.

Share the application, estimated load or requested kVA, dates and worksite location. Maruti Enterprise will confirm whether the requested capacity and service scope can be supported before you make arrangements.

What to check before hiring a DG set

  • List the equipment that will run at the same time and note motor starting loads
  • Confirm whether the requirement is standby, temporary or continuous operation
  • Describe site access, placement and electrical connection requirements
  • Ask about current capacity, model specifications, acoustic enclosure and availability

Request capacity and availability

The capacity selector lists request options from 10 kVA to 1,500 kVA. A listed size is not a statement of fleet inventory; current availability and suitability are confirmed for each enquiry.
