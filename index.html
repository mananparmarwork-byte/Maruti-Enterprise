Temporary power for your site

A useful generator rental enquiry starts with the job to be done. Tell us what needs power, where the equipment will be used, the expected dates and the capacity you are considering.

Maruti Enterprise discusses temporary power requirements for business operations, construction sites, events and commercial facilities across India. Service reach, equipment suitability and availability are confirmed for each enquiry.

What to include in a DG set rental enquiry

  • Your site location and the equipment or activity that needs power
  • Requested kVA capacity, or the electrical load if you need help choosing
  • Required start and end dates, quantity and site access considerations
  • Any installation, operator, servicing or maintenance support to discuss
