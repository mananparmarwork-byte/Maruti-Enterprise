Plan a DG set maintenance contract

An AMC discussion should begin with the generator make, model, capacity, operating hours, usage pattern, service records and site location.

Before a contract is agreed, confirm the service schedule, included work, exclusions, parts handling, response expectations and commercial terms in writing. This website does not promise a fixed visit frequency or response time.

Preventive maintenance for business sites

Factories, warehouses, commercial properties and other sites can describe how the generator is used and what continuity requirements matter. The proposed maintenance plan depends on the unit and the agreed service scope.
