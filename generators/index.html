Choosing a generator for your application

Capacity selection depends on the connected load, starting currents, operating pattern, site conditions and whether the requirement is standby or continuous. Share an equipment list or load estimate if you have one.

The enquiry form accepts requested capacities from 10 kVA to 1,500 kVA, including 40 kVA, 62.5 kVA and 82.5 kVA options. These are request sizes only; exact models, specifications, suitability and availability must be confirmed.

Silent and industrial generator enquiries

If your site needs an acoustic enclosure, a particular phase or voltage, or a specific emission standard, include that in your enquiry. Confirm the exact specification for the selected capacity before making a decision.
